# Glossary: Hydraulic Modelling

## Demand-driven analysis (DDA)

A hydraulic analysis approach in which nodal demand is specified independently of the pressure available at the node.

## Pressure-driven analysis (PDA)

A hydraulic analysis approach in which delivered demand depends on the pressure available at a node.

## Hydraulic head

The sum of elevation head, pressure head, and velocity head at a point in a fluid system.

## Water distribution network (WDN)

A network of pipes, junctions, pumps, valves, tanks, and reservoirs used to convey and distribute water.

## Water Network Tool for Resilience (WNTR)

A Python package for modelling and analysing water distribution network resilience and related hydraulic scenarios.
